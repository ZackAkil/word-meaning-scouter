Why Pre-Scout Vocabulary? (Cognitive Load Theory)

Encountering unfamiliar words mid-sentence forces your working memory to decode vocabulary and track the narrative at the same time—a dual task that easily triggers cognitive overload and breaks reading flow. By segmenting word learning before you read a chapter, you manage and optimise intrinsic cognitive load: you master the tricky vocabulary first, freeing your full mental capacity to comprehend and enjoy the story itself.
